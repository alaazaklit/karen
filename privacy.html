Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

Karen K Sanctuaries (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data we collect

  • Enquiry data — name, email, telephone number, project location, project type, approximate size, the description you share, any plans or images you attach, and a preferred date and time for a call if you request one.
  • Technical data — basic device and usage information required to serve the website securely (for example IP address and browser type).

2. How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange consultations.
  • To prepare for your consultation with relevant project context.
  • To maintain the security and performance of the website.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Legal basis

We process personal data to take steps at your request prior to entering an engagement, and on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating and improving the studio’s services, in accordance with applicable UAE data protection law (including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

4. Retention

Enquiry data is retained for as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period thereafter, unless a longer period is required for an active engagement or by law.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers who help us operate the website and communications (for example hosting and email), bound by confidentiality — and where required by law.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or object to its processing, by contacting info@karenksanctuaries.com.

7. Cookies and storage

This website uses only essential, functional browser storage required for the site to operate — including a session flag that remembers whether the homepage introduction has already played. No third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the latest version will always be posted on this page with its revision date.
